Who I Am

Built by hustle,
backed by credentials

I didn't follow a straight path. I started by teaching school in the mornings while studying pre-engineering in college afternoons. I funded my university degree by working night shifts at a call center, selling duct cleaning services to international clients. Rest in the morning, study in the afternoon, work at night — every day for a year.

That relentless drive eventually landed me in supply chain, where I found my domain. Over the next seven years I managed complete import-export cycles for military-grade materials shipped to Thales LAS France and Juhunges T2M Belgium — one of the most demanding supply chain environments in the world.

At a mining and ores company, I owned the entire operation: sourcing chrome ore and fluoride ore locally and from Afghanistan, exporting to China and Japan, managing inventory, purchase finance, and every transaction record. I built Power BI dashboards that tracked P&L down to individual contracts and shipments — and those insights changed how the CEO ran the business.

I backed that with an MBA in Supply Chain (Silver Medalist, 82.5%) from Bahria University and a CSCP from APICS. Now in Dubai, UAE — open to senior SCM, procurement, and trade operations leadership roles across the GCC.
