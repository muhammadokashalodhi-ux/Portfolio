Who I Am

A supply chain professional
built by real experience

I didn't follow a straight path. I started teaching school in the mornings while studying pre-engineering in college afternoons. I funded my university degree with night-shift call center work. That drive to keep moving is what shaped who I am professionally.

Over 6+ years in supply chain, I've managed complete import-export cycles for defence-grade materials shipped to clients including Thales LAS France and Juhunges T2M Belgium — some of the most demanding supply chain environments in the world.

At a mining and ores company, I owned the entire supply chain: sourcing chrome ore and fluoride ore locally and from Afghanistan, exporting to China and Japan, managing inventory and purchase finance, and building Power BI dashboards that revealed contract-level P&L insights that changed executive strategy.

I backed that with an MBA in Supply Chain (Silver Medalist, 82.5%) from Bahria University and a CSCP certification from APICS. Now based in Dubai, UAE — seeking senior SCM, procurement, or trade operations roles in the GCC.
